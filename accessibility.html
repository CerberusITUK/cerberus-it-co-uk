Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 12 August 2026

1. Our commitment

Cerberus IT is committed to making this website accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA where possible.

2. What we do

  • Use semantic HTML and ARIA landmarks to support screen readers.
  • Provide text alternatives for non-decorative images.
  • Ensure sufficient colour contrast between text and background.
  • Make all interactive elements operable by keyboard.
  • Design layouts that adapt to different screen sizes and zoom levels.
  • Label form fields clearly and associate error messages with inputs.

3. Known limitations

Some areas of the site may not yet fully meet accessibility standards, including:

  • Auto-playing video content may not have a visible control on all devices.
  • Some decorative animations may cause discomfort for users sensitive to motion.
  • Third-party content (e.g. social media icons) may have its own accessibility limitations.

We are actively working to address these issues.

4. Browser compatibility

This site is designed to work with the latest versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. For the best experience, please use a current browser.

5. Feedback and contact

If you experience any accessibility barriers on this site, please let us know. Email support@cerberus-it.co.uk or call 02032 876321, and we will do our best to provide the information you need in an accessible format.

6. Changes to this statement

We may update this statement as we improve the accessibility of our site. Changes will be posted here with an updated revision date.
