About Cerberus IT

Built on trust. Driven by protection.

Our story

Founded in 2004 and based in the UK, Cerberus IT partners with organisations of all sizes to keep their technology secure, accessible, and productive. We believe IT should be a business enabler — not a source of frustration. That's why we combine proactive monitoring, strategic guidance, and genuinely friendly engineers who care about the outcome.

Over the years we've grown alongside our clients, expanding from break-fix support into a full managed IT partner offering cyber security, cloud services, strategic projects, and compliance expertise. We're proud to support businesses across the UK — from growing startups to established organisations with complex needs.

What we believe

  • People first. Technology is only as good as the people behind it. Our engineers are approachable, skilled, and genuinely care about your business.
  • Proactive, not reactive. We monitor, maintain, and plan ahead so issues are resolved before you even notice them.
  • Security by default. Every service we deliver has security built in — not bolted on as an afterthought.
  • Plain English. No jargon, no condescension. We explain things clearly so you can make informed decisions.
  • Long-term partnership. We're not here for a quick fix. We build relationships that last.

How we work

Our approach is built around four pillars:

  • Friendly engineers — Support across the UK, named contacts for every client, and a human-first approach.
  • Proactive monitoring — 24/7 alerting and maintenance to catch issues before they become problems.
  • Strategic guidance — Quarterly roadmap sessions, vCIO services, and IT planning aligned to your business.
  • Trusted & certified — ISO certifications, partner badges, and a commitment to quality and security.

Who we support

We work with growing businesses (4–20 people), established organisations (20+), and in-house IT teams who need co-managed support. Our clients span professional services, financial services, the charitable sector, technology, and more. Whatever your sector, we tailor our services to fit.
