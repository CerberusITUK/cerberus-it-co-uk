Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026

1. Who we are

Cerberus IT ("we", "us", "our") is a managed IT and cyber security provider based in the United Kingdom. You can contact us at support@cerberus-it.co.uk or on 02032 876321.

2. What we collect

We may collect and process the following personal data:

  • Contact details — name, email address, phone number, and company name you provide via our contact form or email.
  • Communication records — copies of correspondence you send us, including support requests.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, and pages visited, collected via standard server logs and analytics tools.
  • Service data — account and system information required to deliver IT support under contract.

3. How we use your data

We process your personal data for the following purposes:

  • To respond to enquiries and provide IT support services.
  • To send you service updates, invoices, and contractual communications.
  • To improve our website, services, and customer experience.
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Legal basis for processing

Under the UK GDPR, we rely on the following lawful bases:

  • Consent — where you submit the contact form or subscribe to communications.
  • Contract — where processing is necessary to deliver services under agreement.
  • Legal obligation — where we must retain records to comply with law.
  • Legitimate interests — for service improvement and security, balanced against your rights.

5. How long we keep data

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes above, or as required by law. Contact form submissions are retained for up to 24 months unless an ongoing service relationship exists.

6. Sharing your data

We may share data with trusted third-party service providers (e.g. email delivery, hosting, or ticketing tools) who act as processors under our instruction. We do not share your data for marketing purposes.

7. Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention).
  • Object to or restrict processing.
  • Request data portability.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

To exercise any of these rights, email support@cerberus-it.co.uk.

8. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit, access controls, and regular security reviews. No method of transmission over the internet is fully secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
