Cookies Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and to provide information to site owners. This policy explains how Cerberus IT uses cookies on this website.

2. How we use cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential cookies — required for the website to function (e.g. remembering your contact form input).
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it.
  • Preference cookies — remember choices you make (e.g. accessibility settings).

We do not use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking.

3. Types of cookies we use

CookiePurposeDuration
Essential session Enables core site functionality Session
Analytics Anonymous usage statistics Up to 12 months
Preference Stores user preferences Up to 12 months

4. Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Each browser manages cookies differently — see the help menu in your browser for details. Disabling cookies may affect functionality of this website.

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
