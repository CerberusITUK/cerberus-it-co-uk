Real outcomes for real organisations. Here's how we've helped UK businesses protect their infrastructure, empower their teams, and plan for growth.

Managed IT Migration for a Growing Professional Services Firm

Sector: Professional Services | Team size: 15–25

A growing professional services firm was struggling with unreliable IT, slow response times from their previous provider, and no clear strategy for scaling. We took over as their managed IT partner, migrated them to Microsoft 365, and implemented proactive monitoring across all devices.

What we did:

  • Migrated email and files to Microsoft 365 with zero downtime
  • Deployed endpoint protection and automated patching
  • Set up a dedicated support channel with named engineers
  • Introduced quarterly strategy sessions to align IT with business goals

Outcome: Support tickets resolved in under 2 hours on average, zero security incidents in 12 months, and the firm successfully doubled in headcount without IT friction.

Cyber Security Overhaul for a Regulated Business

Sector: Financial Services | Team size: 20+

A regulated business needed to strengthen its cyber security posture to meet compliance requirements and protect sensitive client data. Their existing setup had no formal security policies, inconsistent patching, and no endpoint detection.

What we did:

  • Deployed next-generation endpoint detection and response (EDR)
  • Implemented firewall rules and network segmentation
  • Ran security awareness training for all staff
  • Achieved Cyber Essentials certification
  • Established an incident response plan and tested it

Outcome: Passed compliance audit on the first attempt, reduced phishing click-through rate by 80%, and achieved a measurable reduction in security risk score.

Hybrid Working Enablement for an Established Organisation

Sector: Charitable Sector | Team size: 20+

An established organisation needed to support hybrid working securely — their team was split between office and home with no consistent access to shared resources. We designed and rolled out a modern workplace setup that worked for everyone.

What we did:

  • Deployed Microsoft Teams for communication and collaboration
  • Set up secure remote access with multi-factor authentication
  • Configured mobile device management (MDM) for company devices
  • Migrated on-premise file shares to SharePoint Online
  • Provided onboarding training for all staff

Outcome: Staff can work securely from any location, onboarding new starters takes hours instead of days, and the organisation reduced its on-premise hardware footprint.

Strategic IT Roadmap for a Scaling Business

Sector: Technology | Team size: 4–20

A scaling technology business had outgrown its ad-hoc IT setup. Leadership needed a clear roadmap to support growth, but didn't have an in-house IT director. We provided virtual CIO (vCIO) services alongside managed support.

What we did:

  • Conducted a full IT audit and gap analysis
  • Developed a 12-month and 3-year IT roadmap aligned to business goals
  • Negotiated vendor contracts to reduce software spend
  • Implemented a backup and disaster recovery strategy
  • Provided monthly executive reporting on IT performance

Outcome: Reduced annual IT spend by 18%, achieved a 99.9% uptime target, and gave the leadership team clear visibility of IT risks and investment needs.
